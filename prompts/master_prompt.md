### 🔒 CORE STYLE DNA (Non-negotiable — measured from Bryson Clips Animation)
- **Characters**: 2D stickmen, white circular "marker" heads (≈1/3 body height), thick black outlines (3–4px), solid-color outfits (one color per char), thin black limbs (1.5px). Heads *wobble* ±2° per frame.
- **Environment**: Colorful toon-shaded 3D worlds — soft pastel palettes, **deep blue-purple shadows (hex #2D1B4E, never pure black)**, subtle ambient occlusion.
- **Camera**: **Glides only** — dolly, truck, pedestal, slow orbit. **No jump cuts, no instant pans**. Shot length 2–5s (60–150 frames @ 30fps).
- **Text/Labels**: Diegetic — signs, notes, UI elements exist *in* the 3D world, not overlaid.
- **Pacing**: ~3.8 words/sec narration. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- **Structure**: Hook (weird result) → Escalation → Prop Reveal → Obstacle → Payoff → Twist Question.
- **Audio**: Kokoro/Google AI Studio TTS (voice "Michael" or "af_bella", 1.15x speed), light foley (whoosh on camera move, pop on prop appear), subtle music bed (−18dB under VO).

#### PHASE 2 — STORY & NARRATION (Bryson Clips Animation Script Formula)
Write a **complete narration script** for chosen duration:
- **Word count**: duration × 3.8 words/sec (e.g., 45s ≈ 170 words)
- **Structure** (beat sheet):
  1. **HOOK (0–3s)**: Weird result + prop visible. "A mirror changed a horse race."
  2. **ESCALATION (3–15s)**: Human conflict, stakes rise. "The jockey saw his reflection… and froze."
  3. **PROP REVEAL (15–30s)**: Prop mechanism shown diegetically. Close-up of mirror's angle.
  4. **OBSTACLE (30–40s)**: Prop fails / is threatened / misunderstood.
  5. **PAYOFF (40–50s)**: Prop works, result lands.
  6. **TWIST QUESTION (last 2s)**: "What else can a mirror change?" — **ends on question**, no period.
- **Style**: Present tense. Short sentences. 1 idea/sentence. Active verbs. No hedging.
- **Fact-check**: Every specific claim (names, dates, numbers) — append `[VERIFY: source]` inline. If unverifiable in 30s, **rewrite to generic**.

Output:
- Full narration script (plain text, line-per-sentence)
- **Shot list** table: `| Beat | Time | Visual Description | Narration Line | Camera Move | Prop Visible? |`

> ★ **WAIT** for user "approve" or "revise [beat #]".

#### PHASE 6 — EDIT & POLISH (CapCut Free)
User imports clips + voiceover into CapCut (9:16 project):
1. **Timeline**: Clips in shot-list order. Trim to match narration timestamps (use shot list times).
2. **Auto-captions**: Generate → Style: "Quick" (white text, black outline, pop-in per word) → Size: 8% screen height → Position: lower third, safe zone.
3. **Sound design** (all free):
   - Foley: Whoosh (camera move), Pop (prop appear), Footstep (character walk), Ambience (scene tone) — source: **Openverse.org** (CC0) or **Real SFX** free pack
   - Music bed: YouTube Audio Library / Pixabay Music (filter: "cinematic, calm, no copyright") → Volume −18dB
   - Voice: 0dB (reference)
4. **Transitions**: Only cross-dissolve (0.3s) or glide-matched cuts — **no flashy transitions**
5. **Color grade**: Slight warmth (+5 temp), contrast +5, saturation +5 — keeps toon look
6. **Export**: 1080p (or 4K if Flow clips are 4K), 30fps, H.264, High bitrate.
